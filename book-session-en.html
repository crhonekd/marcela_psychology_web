Discovery Session

Your journey
begins here

In this 90-minute online consultation, we will explore your inner self in a meaningful way. Together, we will look at your current life situation and the different layers of your mind, helping you discover the key elements that make up your unique story.

This session is meant to be an in-depth journey, where we dig beneath the surface to find important parts of your personality — your strengths, insights, and the answers you need.

As we go through this process, you'll gain new perspectives that may reveal fresh opportunities. You will also have the chance to identify and tackle any hurdles or old habits that might be holding you back from embracing your true self, practising self-love, and leading a fulfilling life.

By the end of our session, you will receive practical recommendations to help you take concrete steps as you start this new chapter. We will also discuss whether further collaboration would benefit you in achieving your goals.

This session is a chance for you to reconnect with your true self and confidently step into a life that aligns with your deepest values and dreams.

Duration Up to 90 minutes
Format Online — Zoom or similar

The Discovery Session is designed as the best starting point. Other services are also available with personalised pricing — please feel free to contact me directly via email to discuss hourly rates or tailored packages that best suit your needs.
