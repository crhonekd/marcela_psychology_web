How I work

My Approach

I work with people who want to understand themselves more deeply and create lasting psychological change, not temporary symptom management.

My approach combines evidence-based psychology, neuroscience-informed understanding of the nervous system, emotional awareness, and practical therapeutic work that can be integrated into everyday life.

If you want to understand the patterns that no longer serve you, develop a safer and more stable relationship with yourself, and create space for a life that feels more aligned, calm, and authentic — then this is the right space for you.

Change happens in a collaborative process, grounded in both science and human experience.
