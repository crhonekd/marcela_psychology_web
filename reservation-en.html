Cancellation policy

Any cancellation will result in a total loss of the € 500 EUR deposit.

Cancellations made more than six weeks before the retreat begins will receive a 50% refund, excluding the non-refundable deposit.

Cancellations made within the final six weeks before the retreat begins are non-refundable, as accommodation, venue, staffing, and other retreat costs will have already been committed.

We reserve the right to cancel the trip if it is not meeting the trip minimum. (If we do so, you will be refunded in full.)

Should the exchange rate vary more than 5%, the price will be subject to change. Trip cancellation insurance is always recommended whenever making travel plans.

If we find that the trip is not a good fit for you, we reserve the right to cancel your reservation, in which case your payments will be fully refunded.

We appreciate your understanding and commitment. We can't wait to welcome you on this transformative journey!
